About Mehak

Small-town Haryana. A decade in startups. Now, the stage.

Mehak Madan's comedic lens is that of a thirty-something married woman navigating life in India's tech capital — sharp, clean, and disarmingly true. Six years of open mics and shows nationwide, distilled into sets that land.

Book Mehak Watch her work

Before embracing stand-up full-time, I spent a decade immersed in the startup world — and my material draws heavily from those rich experiences.

The standups, the reorgs, the "quick syncs" that weren't — I lived them. That's why a corporate room feels like home turf: I'm not a comedian visiting your workplace, I'm someone who's sat exactly where your audience is sitting.

Originating from a small city in Haryana, I bring a wealth of anecdotes about chasing aspirations from small-town life to the bustling metros. Six years and a hundred-plus events later, the throughline is simple — comedy that's clean, specific, and true. Serious about funny.

The journey

How you get from a Haryana town to a corporate stage.

The beginning

Small-town Haryana

A small city, big aspirations, and an early education in the gap between where you're from and where you're headed — the raw material for a lot of what lands on stage now.

A decade

Inside the startup world

Ten years in the thick of India's startup boom — teams, tools, town halls and the particular comedy of modern work. This is where the corporate set was really written.

Six years & counting

On the mic, full-time

Open mics to headline sets across the country — 100+ events, 60+ of them corporate. A clean, customised style that mixed rooms actually agree on.
