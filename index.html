About Mehak

Small-town Haryana. A decade in startups. Now, the stage.

My comedic lens is that of a thirty-something married woman navigating life in India's tech capital. Before embracing stand-up full-time, I spent ten years immersed in the startup world — and my material draws heavily from those rich experiences.

Originating from a small city in Haryana, I bring a wealth of anecdotes about chasing aspirations from small-town life to the bustling metros — six years of open mics and shows nationwide, distilled into sets that land.
