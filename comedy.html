    Good questions

    Booking a corporate comedian, answered.

    Is Mehak Madan's corporate comedy clean and workplace-safe?

    Yes. Every set is clean by default and custom-written for your company, then pressure-tested against your culture before the date. Across 60+ corporate shows the material stays sharp without the HR headaches.

    What corporate comedy formats does she offer?

    One performer, eight formats: stand-up comedy (clean, custom, or a gentle roast), hosting and anchoring, keynotes, learning and team-building sessions, panel moderation, on-stage stakeholder interviews, and full event messaging.

    Which cities does she perform in?

    Mehak is based in Bengaluru (Bangalore) and performs nationwide across India — town halls, offsites, annual days and conferences.

    What languages does she perform in?

    English, Hindi, or a comfortable mix — matched to your room.

    How long is a corporate comedy show?

    A 60-minute set by default, with 45 or 90 minutes available on request.

    How much does it cost to book a corporate comedian in India?

    It depends on the format, city, date and audience size — a 60-minute set is priced differently from full-day hosting. Share your event in the booking form and you'll get a clear quote within two working days.

    What topics does the set cover?

    Modern working life: AI at work, generational differences (Gen Z vs Millennial vs Gen X), the life of a manager, operations vs tech, work-life balance, therapy, social media, and changing workplaces and the role of women — all customisable to your company.

    Can the show be customised to our company?

    Yes — that's the point. The anecdotes are mined from your company, your quarter and the meetings your people just sat through, so it feels less like a comedian visiting and more like someone who's been on your Slack the whole time.

    What do you need on the day?

    Just a mic and a stage. No slides, no setup tax. The show works for rooms from 20 to 2,000.

    How far in advance should we book?

    Earlier is better for date availability, but reach out anytime — you'll hear back within a day with availability and ideas for the show.
