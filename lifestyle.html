One trusted person handles your calendar and inbox, travel and itineraries, follow-through on the commitments you said you'd keep, and vendor, errand and household coordination — so you get your week and your attention back.

Founders and executives in Bengaluru whose time is their most expensive asset and who are done managing their own logistics at 11pm.

Completely. It works by referral and consultation, with a single point of contact and discretion and NDAs as standard.

It's broader and more personal — one consistent person across your calendar, travel, follow-through and household, rather than a rotating team or a purely in-office EA.
